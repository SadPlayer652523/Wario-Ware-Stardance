# Controls

Move: Left and Right arrow keys
Look down: Down arrow key
Jump: D key or Spacebar
Debug Mode: S key
Air dash: D key or Spacebar in the air

# Minigame Summaries:

## Minigame 1:

- Collect all Godot icons in 10 seconds to continue to the next minigame
- If timer runs out, you lose 1 life
- Use the air dash to get a faster time!

## Minigame 2:

- Click all Godot icons in 7 seconds to continue
- Try to click them as fast as you can!
- Timer runs out = -1 life

## Minigame 3:

- Click the Godot icon 100 times in 15 seconds to continue
- Hint: Use mouse keys to get an extremely fast time
- Timer runs out = -1 life

## Minigame 4:

- Dodge the red hazards and make it to the end in 4 seconds!
- Hint: No need for fancy dodging
- Get hit by hazard = die and -1 life

That's it!
